# Validation and reproducibility

This document records checks from the completed, validated analysis of COTSI's historical Facebook SMS-verification offers. It documents the accounting and sample construction; it does not independently verify the truth of the vendors' advertised offers.

## Source and prepared data

The input was the authors' `verifications data_final.zip` replication archive. Its validated local SHA-256 is `0acd5daf991033022c19f6276e4e4d5ea05a61bd564a903cf7fef4bbdd1f3b2d`. All **12 intended monthly CSV members** from August 2024 through July 2025 were read in chunks, totaling **9,960,721 raw rows**. The fixed Facebook/GB–US–ID/date filter retained **1,080 country-platform-date rows** (360 per market). The parser kept identifiers as text, including the literal country code `NA`, and found no required-field, target-date, numeric-parsing, month/date or source-key failures.

The four vendor records per observed source row produced **4,320 observed vendor-day records**. An explicit daily calendar added the missing **30 December 2024** in each market, giving **4,332 calendar-complete vendor-day rows**. The prepared CSV was re-read with no duplicate country-platform-vendor-date keys. Its validated SHA-256 is `0eca581c1e37ed2df40e7750bb40a13e275c5dd8dea5028f229d1563f886b8c2`.

## Reconstruction and eligibility

Observed vendor stocks were finite and nonnegative; positive-stock vendors had finite positive recorded-USD prices; recorded RUB conversion factors were finite and positive. The four vendor stocks reconstructed the source's combined stock with **zero failures**. Vendor stock shares summed to one on valid observed days within `1e-12`. The reconstructed stock-weighted price matched the source's recorded combined `priceUSD` with **zero failures** under `1e-10 + 1e-8 × |source price|`. The source's recorded RUB-per-USD exchange rate was used; no external replacement rate was introduced. The data-preparation audit recorded **53 PASS, 6 INFO and 0 FAIL** checks.

Calendar lags were formed only after adding missing dates and sorting within market/vendor. The first date lacks a previous-day pair. The missing 30 December prevents both the pair ending that day and the pair ending **31 December**; **29 and 31 December are not compared directly**. Each market has **358** valid adjacent-day pairs. Eligibility requires the same positive-stock vendor set of at least two vendors on both days:

Changing-set pairs remain in the prepared data for the selection check but are not decomposed by assigning absent vendor prices.

## Decomposition and saved outputs

R checked the prepared CSV's fingerprint, types, keys, calendar coverage, reconstructed-measure flags and country-specific pair counts. On **all 1,001 eligible pairs**, the symmetric price component plus stock-share weight component equalled the observed recorded weighted-price change within the documented numerical tolerance, `2e-10 + 1e-8 × (|today's source price| + |previous day's source price|)`. There were **zero identity failures**; the maximum absolute residual was **`3.885781e-16` recorded USD per verification**. The daily, country-summary and vendor-set-selection tables re-read with **1,001, 3 and 6 rows**, respectively. Both figures were saved and visually reviewed, including the missing-date markers, vendor-set-change annotations and retained July observations.

## Reproduction boundary

A fresh-session run of the validated Python → R → Quarto workflow regenerated the prepared CSV, audit, three tables, two figures and HTML report without an analytical discrepancy. The public scripts retain the same filters, calculations, eligibility rules, formulas and tolerances; only repository paths, comments and presentation/audit labels were adapted. A separate temporary run of these **public-path script copies** against the unchanged local archive reproduced the processed CSV's exact validated hash, the **53 PASS / 6 INFO / 0 FAIL** audit counts, the **1,001-pair / zero-failure** decomposition result, and byte-identical copies of all three tables and two figures. Windows command-line R emitted locale-startup warnings (`C.UTF-8` could not be set), but no analytical warning or output discrepancy. The temporary source link and generated intermediates were not included in this repository.

Run the commands in the [README](../README.md) after placing the matching source archive locally. Python/R regenerate data and results; Quarto embeds the regenerated figures. Numerical tables and prose in `report/report.qmd` are manually maintained and must be checked against the regenerated CSVs before rendering. Rendering alone does not refresh those numbers.

## Limits of validation

These checks establish that the selected archive was processed consistently and that the accounting identity holds for eligible pairs. They cannot establish that advertised stock equals sales, genuine availability or unique SIM cards, or that recorded prices were realised costs of successful verification. The abrupt July 2025 SMSHub recorded-USD quotes passed arithmetic checks but could reflect an upstream reporting issue. Vendor quality/product mix may differ, the selected phone-number markets are not representative, adjacent pairs are temporally dependent, and changing-set pairs are outside the decomposition. The results are descriptive and non-causal.
